## How to choose

- **Billing model.** Per minute, per call, per unique caller, or a monthly price set to your volume. Work out your busiest month, not your average one.
- **Hold time.** A live service shares its operators across clients, so callers can wait on hold at busy times. An AI receptionist answers every call at once.
- **Emergency rules.** Check who decides what counts as urgent, and how the on-call person is reached (a text is easy to sleep through; a phone call is not).
- **Integrations.** Does it book into your CRM, field service or property management software, or does someone retype every message?
- **Setup.** Self-serve tools know what you configure. Done-for-you services build and tune the agent for you.
